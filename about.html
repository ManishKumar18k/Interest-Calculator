How Our Calculator Works

Our calculator uses a simple interest calculation method. Users enter the principal amount, monthly rate per ₹100, start date and end date.

Interest = Amount × Rate × Days ÷ 3000

The calculation is based on a 30-day month. The calculator displays the interest amount and total amount so users can understand the result easily.
