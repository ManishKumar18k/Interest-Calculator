LEARN THE BASICS

What is Simple Interest?

Simple interest is the interest calculated only on the original principal amount. It does not include previously earned interest in the calculation.

It is commonly used for understanding basic interest calculations on money borrowed or invested over a period of time.

Simple Interest Formula

Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) ÷ 100

When calculating interest using a monthly rate per ₹100 and the number of days, this calculator uses:

Interest = Amount × Monthly Rate × Days ÷ 3000
CALCULATION EXAMPLE

Simple Interest Example

Let's understand the calculation with an example.

Principal Amount ₹10,000
Monthly Rate ₹2 per ₹100
Time Period 30 Days
Interest ₹200
Total Amount ₹10,200

How is it calculated?

Principal = ₹10,000
Rate = ₹2 per ₹100 per month
Time = 30 days

₹10,000 × 2 × 30 ÷ 3000 = ₹200

Therefore, the interest is ₹200 and the total amount is ₹10,200.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Interest Calculator?

An interest calculator is a tool that helps you calculate interest on a principal amount based on a rate and time period.

Is this calculator free to use?

Yes, this calculator is free to use. You do not need to create an account or register.

How is interest calculated?

Interest is calculated using the principal amount, monthly rate per ₹100 and the number of days. The calculation assumes a 30-day month.

Does this calculator calculate compound interest?

No. This calculator currently calculates simple interest. Compound interest calculations are different because interest may be added to the principal over time.

Can I use this calculator on my mobile?

Yes. The website is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Do I need to download an application?

No. You can use this calculator directly in your web browser.

Is this calculator financial advice?

No. This calculator is only a calculation tool. It does not provide financial, investment or legal advice. Please verify your rate and calculation before making decisions.
